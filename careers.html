Build your career

We are a family-run firm that still builds the old way — on site, by hand, with an obsessive eye for finish. If you would rather learn construction standing in it than reading about it, you will fit in here.

Why Sagar

What working here
is actually like.

i.

Learn it standing in it

Junior engineers here run pours, check steel and walk slabs with the partners — not from a head-office desk. You will know every project by its staircase.

ii.

Small teams, real responsibility

We are deliberately compact. That means your decisions reach the site the same week, and your name is on the work — for better, which keeps standards honest.

iii.

Three generations of mentorship

Fifty-five years of built work sits in the same office as you. The fastest way to learn this trade is beside people who have already made its mistakes.
