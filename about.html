About the firm

Fifty-five years
on site.

Sagar Construction Co. was founded in 1971 in Mumbai by Shri Vidyasagar Melaram Gupta — a builder who believed a home should outlast the hand that built it. Five and a half decades later, that's still the only standard we work to.

The founder

Shri Vidyasagar Melaram Gupta

In 1971, Shri Vidyasagar Melaram Gupta took on Sagar Construction's first commission: Panchratna, near Opera House — the firm's founding and, to this day, its flagship project. There was no second office, no back-up crew — just a plan, a plot, and a promise to the family who'd bought into it. That project still stands, and so does the company he built around it.

Over the following decades he took the firm from single residential blocks into cinema halls, commercial plates, and entire residential colonies — carrying the business from South Mumbai's Girgaon and Opera House out to Ghatkopar, Chembur, and eventually New Panvel, laying the groundwork for the company his family runs today.

Milestones

How we got here

1971

Sagar Construction Co. is founded

Shri Vidyasagar Melaram Gupta delivers Panchratna near Opera House — the firm's first and flagship project, and the beginning of a Mumbai-wide practice.

1972 – 1979

South Mumbai and Ghatkopar

Ridge Apartments rises on Malabar Hill alongside the Odeon Theatre and Odeon Apartments in Girgaon, while Vora and Arihant Apartments mark the firm's entry into Ghatkopar.

1988 – 1995

Expansion east and inward

Rajnigandha Apartments and Abhilash open up New Panvel and Chembur, followed by Pleasant Park back in central Mumbai.

2000 – 2002

A cluster of family housing

Prerna, Abhinav I & II, and Utsav Apartments are built in close succession across Mumbai and Panvel — the firm's busiest residential stretch to date.

2010

Beyond the city: Lonavala Villas

The firm's first hill-station development, built into the Sahyadris outside Mumbai — a departure from apartment blocks into standalone villas.

2024 – 2028

Raj Vihar completed, Emperia C2 underway

Raj Vihar is completed and delivered in 2024. The next generation of the family now takes the firm into its largest development yet — Emperia C2, built in joint venture and targeted for completion by December 2028.
