Timeline

Five decades,
one city.

1971

Where it began

Shri Vidyasagar Melaram Gupta founds the firm; among its earliest commissions is Panchratna, near Opera House — the beginning of a Mumbai-wide practice.

1970s–80s

South Mumbai

Ridge Apartments, the Odeon blocks, Vellard View — the firm builds its name across Malabar Hill, Girgaon and Mahim.

1990s–2000s

Eastward

Ghatkopar, Chembur and New Panvel: family housing for a growing city, including the Abhinav and Rajnigandha blocks.

2010s

Hillside

Lonavala Villas — weekend homes in the Sahyadris, the firm's first project outside Mumbai.

2024–2028

The next generation

The next generation of the family now takes the firm into its next major development — Emperia C2, built in joint venture and targeted for completion by December 2028.
