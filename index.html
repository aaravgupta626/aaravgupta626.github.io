Where is Sagar Construction Co. based?

Our office is at Nana Chowk, 801 Autcommerce House, Kennedy Bridge, Mumbai 400007. We have built across South Mumbai, Ghatkopar, Chembur, New Panvel and Lonavala since 1971.

How do I enquire about a project?

Write to info@sagarconstruction.example, call +91 91373 24613, or tap the WhatsApp button on any page. Each project in the register also has its own WhatsApp and email enquiry buttons.

Do you develop in joint venture?

Yes. Recent developments including Emperia C2 and Raj Vihar are joint-venture projects, and we continue to partner on select developments across Mumbai.

How do I apply for a job?

Visit our Careers page — tell us where you fit in, or write directly to careers@sagarconstruction.example with your resume.
